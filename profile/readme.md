# LingoVille Language Centre (Sfax)
  
<img src="https://cert.lingo-ville.com/logo.png" style="align: center; max-width: 50%;">


Welcome to the github for **LingoVille Language Centre**, an innovative training hub located in **Sfax, Tunisia**. This centre specializes in dynamic language instruction and introductory IT skills tailored for children and young adolescents.

## 🚀 Core Programs & Activities

* **Language Learning:** Immersive and communicative tracks in **English** and **French**.
* **IT Exploration:** Fun, foundational lessons in information technology to prepare young minds for a digital world.
* **Interactive Methodology:** Heavily relies on role-playing, active gamification, and hands-on technological projects instead of rigid memorization.

## 📍 Location & Community

* **City:** Sfax, Tunisia
* **Target Audience:** Kids, teenagers, and young learners seeking real-world fluency and digital literacy.
* **Online Presence:** Check out ongoing student projects, schedules, and camp announcements directly via their official [Instagram Page](https://www.instagram.com/lingovillelanguagecentre/).

## 🛠️ Usage / How to Enroll

1. Visit the center location in Sfax or reach out through their digital recruitment channels.
2. Attend a placement orientation to determine the best interactive tier for your child.
3. Participate in weekly workshops combining linguistic communication with practical tech assignments.
